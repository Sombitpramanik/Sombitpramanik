# About Me:

* **What I'm building:** Currently working on a private Android application using Modern Jetpack Compose that will soon be published.
* **Background & Tech:** Strong foundation in Python (especially Flask) and ML. Published a research paper on cancer detection using machine learning back in my 3rd year of college.
* **Hardware & IoT:** Hands-on experience tinkering with microcontrollers and hardware like ESP32 and Raspberry Pi.
* **Problem Solving:** In my free time, I love building practical solutions for myself and the people closest to me, and sometimes sharing them with everyone else.
* **Personality & Vision:** I am a huge extrovert who genuinely loves talking to new people, learning about different cultures, and building stuff that actually helps out fellow developers and everyday people.
